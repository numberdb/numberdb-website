## Findings, ranked

### 1. Formula (1) is wrong for $K_\nu$

A reader sees: "If $Z_\nu$ is either $I_\nu$ or $K_\nu$, then
$Z_{\nu-1}(x)-Z_{\nu+1}(x)=2\nu Z_\nu(x)/x$ [4]."

For $I_\nu$ that is right, and holds on the stored rows: at
$(\nu,x)=(1,\tfrac74)$, $(\tfrac32,1)$ and $(2,5)$ the residual is below
$10^{-99}$. For $K_\nu$ the sign is the other way:
$K_{\nu-1}(x)-K_{\nu+1}(x)=-2\nu K_\nu(x)/x$. At $\nu=1$, $x=\tfrac74$
the formula as printed leaves a residual of $-0.4468$ with mpmath, and
$2\times10^{-122}$ with the sign flipped. DLMF 10.29.1 states the
recurrence for $Z_\nu$ equal to $I_\nu$ or $e^{\nu\pi i}K_\nu$, and the
transcription dropped the factor. This is the $J$-and-$Y$ recurrence
carried over, where the two functions do satisfy the same relation.

$K_\nu$ is not held here, not defined anywhere on the page, and has no
table in the corpus (a search for "Macdonald" finds nothing and
"modified Bessel" finds only T116, T187, T190). Smallest fix: state
the formula for the function the table holds,
"$I_{\nu-1}(x)-I_{\nu+1}(x)=2\nu I_\nu(x)/x$ [4]", and let $K_\nu$
go. Worth doing: it is the one false statement on the page, and a
reader checking a $K$ value against it would conclude their value was
wrong.

### 2. Thirty rows carry the same comment, and it is formula (3)

Every $\nu=\tfrac12$ row says "$I_{1/2}(x)=\sqrt{2/(\pi x)}\sinh x$.",
thirty times, and formula (3) says the same thing once for all of
them. The skill leaves out a comment that "repeats what a formula in
the document already says for every row at once". The published T187
has the same thirty comments on its $J_{1/2}$ rows, so this is the
family's convention now rather than this table's slip.

The argument for keeping something on the row is real: a search by
number lands on the row, not on the formula, and the comment is what
tells that reader their value is elementary. If a comment stays, make
it the row's own fact, "$=\sqrt{2/\pi}\,\sinh 1$" on the row for
$x=1$, which the formula does not print and which a reader can type
into something else. Marginal, and a decision for all three tables.

### 3. A keyword promises something the page never says

`Keywords` carries "von Mises distribution", which is the first search
weight, so somebody typing that lands here. Nothing on the page
mentions it: the word does not occur in the rendered HTML. The fact
behind the keyword is short and belongs in a comment, because it is
the commonest place a reader outside analysis meets $I_0$: the von
Mises density on the circle is
$e^{\kappa\cos(\theta-\mu)}/(2\pi I_0(\kappa))$, so a reader who
arrived from that normalising constant learns why the row is here.
Smallest fix: one sentence in a comment with a
Wikipedia link for the distribution added to `Links`, or else drop the
keyword. Worth doing, and small.

### 4. Comment (7) says "associated" and the identity it alludes to is not on the page

A reader sees: "Half-integer orders are elementary. The Bessel
polynomials occur in explicit formulas for the associated spherical
and modified spherical functions [7]." Associated with what, a reader
asks, and [7] is DLMF 10.49, which does not name the Bessel
polynomials. Formula (3) gives $I_{1/2}$ only, so "elementary" is
asserted for the $\tfrac32$ and $\tfrac52$ rows and shown for neither.

The relation that is true is one line: for integer $n\geq0$,
$I_{n+1/2}(x)=\bigl(e^{x}\,y_n(-1/x)+(-1)^{n+1}e^{-x}\,y_n(1/x)\bigr)/\sqrt{2\pi x}$,
with $y_n$ the Bessel polynomial of HREF{Bessel_polynomials}. Checked
in balls for $n=0$ to $4$ at $x=\tfrac14,1,\tfrac74,5$: all overlap,
differences below $10^{-114}$. At $n=0$ it is formula (3) and at $n=1$
it is $\sqrt{2/(\pi x)}\,(\cosh x-\sinh x/x)$. Suggested (7): "At
half-integer order $I_\nu$ is $\sqrt{2/(\pi x)}$ times a combination
of $\sinh x$ and $\cosh x$ whose coefficients are polynomials in $1/x$
[7]; in terms of the Bessel polynomials $y_n$, [the identity above]."
The same sentence is on T187 and T190 with "sin" and "cos" and the
$J+iY$ form of the identity, which the T187 critique of 2026-09-09
proposed. Worth doing with the siblings; the present sentences are not
wrong so much as not saying anything a reader can use.

## Noted only

* **The definition is a sentence about the table.** "This table gives
  the real principal values of the modified Bessel function of the
  first kind $I_\nu(x)$ [1] [2], for rational order $\nu\geq0$ and
  rational argument $x>0$" names the function and cites it, with two
  citations back to back, and a reader who does not know what
  $I_\nu$ is learns it from a footnote. The corpus's own Bessel
  polynomials table gives a recurrence and a differential equation
  and stops; here that would be
  $x^2y''+xy'-(x^2+\nu^2)y=0$ with
  $I_\nu(x)=\sum_{k\geq0}(x/2)^{2k+\nu}/(k!\,\Gamma(\nu+k+1))$. The
  published T187 and T190 have exactly this shape as of 2026-09-10, by
  a person's choice, so this is a remark about the family and not a
  request.
* **"For real order $\alpha$"** in the four zero and extrema glosses,
  while T20's own parameter $\alpha$ is typed a complex number and its
  comment restricts the entries to half-integers. "for order $\alpha$"
  would be safe against both. T187 and T190 say the same.
* **Comment (6) writes $\mathrm{i}_n$ and cites DLMF**, whose notation
  is $\mathrm{i}^{(1)}_n$; DLMF 10.47 has two modified spherical
  functions of the first kind, $\mathrm{i}^{(1)}_n$ with $I_{n+1/2}$
  and $\mathrm{i}^{(2)}_n$ with $I_{-n-1/2}$. The formula given is
  10.47.7 and is right (checked at $n=0$ against $\sinh x/x$); only the
  name is Wikipedia's under DLMF's citation.
* **$K_\nu$ appears in (1) and (5) and is never defined or linked.**
  If finding 1 is taken, only (5) names it, beside a DLMF citation,
  which is enough.
* **Rendering the page prints to the server's stdout.** Every render
  of a table writes `label: program-sage` and the whole `properties`
  dict to standard output, from two bare `print` calls at
  `numberdb_app/views.py:755` and `:1114`. Harmless to a reader, noise
  in the container log, and not this table's fault.
