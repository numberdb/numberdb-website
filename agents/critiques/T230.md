## Findings, ranked

### 1. Every imaginary row has a closed form in $\log\Gamma$, and the page gives it for $D=-4$ only (worth doing)

**What a reader sees.** The $D=-4$ row carries
$2\gamma+2\log2+3\log\pi-4\log\Gamma(1/4)$. The $D=-3$ row says only
"$\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$." Nothing on the page says that the other 304
imaginary rows have the same kind of expression.

Who arrives at this table holding a number? Mostly somebody working with
$\Gamma$ at rational arguments: the Chowla-Selberg formula, periods of CM
elliptic curves, Kronecker's limit formula. Their number is an expression in
$\log\Gamma(a/|D|)$, $\gamma$ and $\log 2\pi$. The page shows one example of
that shape and gives no hint that it is general.

**Why it is wrong.** For $D<0$, the functional equation of $L(s,\chi_D)$
relates $L'/L$ at $s=1$ and $s=0$. Lerch's formula then gives
$L'(0,\chi)=-\log|D|\,L(0,\chi)+\sum_a\chi(a)\log\Gamma(a/|D|)$, with
$L(0,\chi_D)=-\frac1{|D|}\sum_a a\,\chi_D(a)$. The $\log|D|$ terms cancel,
which leaves

$$\gamma_K=2\gamma+\log 2\pi+\frac{|D|\sum_{a=1}^{|D|-1}\chi_D(a)\log\Gamma(a/|D|)}{\sum_{a=1}^{|D|-1}a\,\chi_D(a)}\qquad(D<0).$$

The fraction's prefactor is $-w_K/(2h_K)$. This form holds on all 305
imaginary rows to within $4.9\cdot10^{-100}$, and at $D=-4$ it gives the
page's own expression. At $D=-3$ it simplifies, using
$\Gamma(\tfrac13)\Gamma(\tfrac23)=2\pi/\sqrt3$, to
$\gamma_K=2\gamma+4\log2\pi-\tfrac32\log3-6\log\Gamma(1/3)$. $\Gamma(1/3)$
is the other argument everybody meets.

**Smallest fix.**
- Add the displayed identity to `Formulas` as, say, `formula-imaginary`. The
  form above needs nothing the table does not already define except
  $\chi_D$, and the comments define that. If $h_K$ and $w_K$ are preferred,
  they need defining, as T128's comments do.
- Give the $D=-3$ row its expression in the same way as $D=-4$ (see
  finding 4 for the wording).
- The real fields have Kronecker's second limit formula, in terms of
  $\log|\eta|$ at reduced quadratic irrationals. That is much less often what
  a reader holds, and I do not press it.

### 2. No `Links` section, so the Dedekind zeta function, the Kronecker symbol and $\gamma$ are each named and neither linked nor defined (worth doing)

**What a reader sees.** The page has no Links at all. The skill lists Links
among the five sections every table has, and T128, which holds the residues
of these same 607 fields, carries five. Several things go unexplained:

- "its Dedekind zeta function" in the Definition is plain text.
- "the Kronecker symbol" in `comment-quadratic-character` is plain text.
- $\gamma$ appears in `formula-quadratic-l`, in the $D=-4$ comment and in
  `rigour details`, and nothing says what it is. A reader has to reach the
  fourth Similar-tables line ("its row $\gamma_0$ is
  $\gamma_{\mathbb{Q}}$") and infer that $\gamma=\gamma_0$. Nothing says
  that either.

`formula-quadratic-l` also switches to $\zeta_{\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt D)}$ and
$\gamma_{\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt D)}$. Everywhere else the page writes $\zeta_K$ and
$\gamma_K$, with $K=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt D)$ fixed in the Definition, so a reader
checks whether two different objects are meant.

**Why it is wrong.** "Define notation where you use it", and "link the first
mention of a thing to what explains it". The Wikipedia articles exist: I
checked that both `Dedekind_zeta_function` and `Kronecker_symbol` answer 200.
No Wikipedia article on the Euler-Kronecker constant itself exists (404), so
Ihara's paper, already cited, stays the source for the constant.

**Smallest fix.**
- Add `Links` with `Wiki: Wikipedia: Dedekind zeta function` and
  `WikiKronecker: Wikipedia: Kronecker symbol`. T128's two OEIS discriminant
  lists (A003657, A003658) are worth copying too, since they list the
  parameter.
- Put `CITE{Wiki}` after "Dedekind zeta function" in the Definition, and
  `CITE{WikiKronecker}` after "the Kronecker symbol".
- Define $\gamma$ once. One way is to extend `comment-quadratic-character`,
  or add a comment: "$\gamma=\gamma_{\mathbb{Q}}$ is Euler's constant,
  HREF{Stieltjes_constants#0}[$\gamma_0$]."
- In `formula-quadratic-l`, write $\zeta_K(s)=\zeta(s)L(s,\chi_D)$ and
  $\gamma_K=\gamma+L'(1,\chi_D)/L(1,\chi_D)$.

### 3. `comment-log-derivative` says in words what `formula-laurent` says in symbols (worth doing; one deletion)

**What a reader sees.** Comment (1) reads "Equivalently, $\gamma_K$ is the
constant term in $\zeta_K'(s)/\zeta_K(s)+1/(s-1)$ at $s=1$." Formula (2)
reads "$\zeta_K'(s)/\zeta_K(s)=-1/(s-1)+\gamma_K+O(s-1)$ at $s=1$." They are
the same statement. The reader meets it twice and looks for a difference that
is not there.

**Why it is wrong.** Comments hold conventions and caveats, not formulas, and
the formula already has its home.

**Smallest fix.** Delete `comment-log-derivative`. `formula-laurent` stays.

### 4. The $D=-4$ comment is a field and a bare expression joined by a semicolon (worth doing, together with finding 1)

what it equals, so a reader has to guess that it is the value in the row

**Smallest fix.** Write "$\mathbb{Q}(i)$, where

### 5. Two Similar-tables relations do not say what a reader will find there (worth doing for the first two; the others noted)

- **T128**: "lists the residue $c_{-1}$ for the same quadratic fields". T128
  calls that number $\kappa_D$, so a reader who follows the link finds no
  $c_{-1}$. Neither table says that it is $L(1,\chi_D)$, the denominator of
  `formula-quadratic-l`. Better: "lists $\kappa_D=c_{-1}=L(1,\chi_D)$, the
  denominator in CITE{formula-quadratic-l}, for the same 607 fields."
- **T145**: "stores $L(1,\chi)$ for many primitive characters". "Many" is
  conductor $q\leq30$, which reaches only the fields with $|D|\leq30$. There
  the character is indexed by its Conrey label, not by $D$, a point T128's
  own relation to T145's neighbour makes explicitly. Better: "holds
  $L(1,\chi_D)$ for $|D|\leq30$, with $\chi_D$ under its Conrey label
  $(|D|,n)$ rather than under $D$."
- **T34**, noted only: "stores the analogous logarithmic-derivative
  expansion for $\mathbb{Q}$". The fact is sharper: T34 writes
  $\zeta'/\zeta=-\sum\eta_n(s-1)^n$, so its $\eta_0=-\gamma=-\gamma_{\mathbb{Q}}$.
  The sign trips a reader comparing with `formula-laurent`, and the row can
  be linked as `HREF{Laurent_coefficients_of_logarithmic_derivative_of_Riemann_zeta_function_at_1#eta_n,0}`.
- **T159**, noted only: "is the residue table for cubic fields" says what
  T159 is, not how it relates to this table. Either give the relation ("the
  same residue $c_{-1}$, for cubic fields") or drop the line.

denominator but not $L'(1,\chi_D)$, which is the part of the value nothing

Smallest fix:
- name the discriminants the build compared against PARI (or use the six

- write "and $L(1,\chi_D)$ with $\kappa_D$ in HREF{...}[the residues of

the closed form for every $D<0$", which is a real independent check of

  says it and gives the count.
- The parameter line reads "fundamental discriminant ($D$ is a fundamental
  discriminant and $D\ne1$)". The constraint could be just "$D\ne1$".
