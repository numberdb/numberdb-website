## The audit

The audit is clean, and it is right about what it checks: one parameter, a
header true of every row, and links that resolve. It cannot see finding 1,
because that is about what a *different* table defines under the same letter.
It misses the unlinked "satisfiability threshold" in the Definition (finding
2), because the prose words differ from T272's title.

What reads well: the title contains the word a reader would type. The
parameter describes the family ($k\geq3$) and not the run. The range is in
`complete-note`, where it belongs. `rigour details` says how the bracket was
obtained and what it was checked against, and the Formulas contain no
statements about checking. The program works as written.

## Findings, ranked

### 1. $\xi_k$ is the $\ell=1$ column of the roots $\xi_{k,\ell}$ defined in T274, and the page never says so. Worth doing, and it needs a decision before publication.

T274, "Cuckoo hashing thresholds of random $k$-uniform hypergraphs", defines

> $\xi_{k,\ell}$ is the unique positive solution of
> $k\ell=\xi_{k,\ell}Q(\xi_{k,\ell},\ell)/Q(\xi_{k,\ell},\ell+1)$

with $Q(x,s)=\mathbb{P}(\mathrm{Po}(x)\geq s)$. At $\ell=1$,
$Q(x,1)=1-e^{-x}$ and $Q(x,2)=1-e^{-x}-xe^{-x}$. That is exactly T287's
equation $k=\psi(\xi)$, so $\xi_k=\xi_{k,1}$. T274's own rows say so: its
$(3,1)$ comment reads "The root is $\xi_{3,1}=2.14912579991$", the value in
T287's row 3. T274 also says its $\ell=1$ thresholds are T272's.

A reader of Dietzfelbinger et al., the cuckoo-hashing paper T287 already
cites, who arrives holding 2.1491 lands on a page that mentions only XORSAT.
Nothing on the page points to T274. `Keywords` also lacks "cuckoo hashing",
which T272 has.

This leads to a scoping question that only a person can settle. T274 still
has the roots $\xi_{k,\ell}$ for $\ell\geq2$ only as 12-digit numbers in entry
comments. That is the "value belongs in a row" pattern that T287 exists to fix
for $\ell=1$. Two options:

- **(a) Keep T287 as it is**, and add a `Similar tables` entry:
  `HREF{T274}[cuckoo hashing thresholds of random $k$-uniform hypergraphs]`,
  relation "the roots $\xi_{k,\ell}$ there reduce to $\xi_k$ at bucket
  capacity $\ell=1$". Add "cuckoo hashing" to `Keywords`. This is the
  smallest change.
- **(b) Widen T287 to $\xi_{k,\ell}$** with a second parameter $\ell$. It
  would then hold every root the three companion tables leave in comments.
  Parameter keys are frozen at publication, so this is cheap now and
  impossible afterwards.

I would do (a) at least. I am raising (b) because it can only be chosen
while the table is a draft.

### 2. The Definition defines the problem before the number, and it leaves "its satisfiability threshold" unlinked and loosely attached. Worth doing.

The Definition reads:

> For $k\geq3$, random $k$-XORSAT is a system of linear equations over
> $\mathbb{F}_2$, each involving $k$ Boolean variables. The number $\xi_k$ is
> the positive real solution of $k=\ldots$, the auxiliary root used to write
> its satisfiability threshold.

There are three problems:

- **The first sentence is not about $\xi_k$.** It also does not say what makes
  the system random. Nobody needs that sentence to build this table, and T272
  already defines the model properly.
- **"its" is ambiguous.** It reaches back a whole sentence to "random
  $k$-XORSAT", so a reader first tries to attach it to $\xi_k$.
- **"the auxiliary root used to write" is a note to oneself, not a sentence
  of mathematics.** It also misses the first mention of a family the corpus
  holds. The satisfiability threshold is T272, and the Definition does not
  link it.

Smallest fix: "For an integer $k\geq3$, $\xi_k$ is the positive real solution
of $k=\xi(1-e^{-\xi})/(1-e^{-\xi}-\xi e^{-\xi})$. It determines
HREF{T272}[the satisfiability threshold $\alpha_k$ of random $k$-XORSAT]
CITE{WikiXORSAT} through $\alpha_k=\xi_k/(k(1-e^{-\xi_k})^{k-1})$
CITE{PittelSorkin}."

The Definition says "the" solution, but uniqueness is not stated anywhere.
T274 says "unique" for the same equation. $\psi$ appears to increase from
$\psi(0^+)=2$: sampled at 0.001, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 5, 10 and 20 it gives 2.0003,
2.18, 2.39, 2.91, 3.56, 5.18, 10.005 and 20.000001. A clause giving the
reason, or at least the word "unique" as T274 has it, would make the
Definition settle the question. This part is only a note.

### 3. The one Comment repeats a Formula and a Similar-tables entry, and says nothing else. Worth doing; it is a deletion or a replacement.

> The notation and indexing match the satisfiability threshold table:
> $\xi_k$ is the root that gives
> $\alpha_k=\xi_k/(k(1-e^{-\xi_k})^{k-1})$.

- **The formula after the colon is `formula-threshold`, word for word.**
  Comments are not the place for formulas.
- **"The notation and indexing match … table" is a remark about the
  website.** `Similar tables` already makes the connection.

A reader holding 2.149 wants the thing this page never says: what $\xi_k$
*measures*. T272's `formula-core` supplies it. There $x=k\alpha q^{k-1}$ with
$q=1-e^{-x}$, so $x=k\alpha(1-e^{-x})^{k-1}$. At $\alpha=\alpha_k$ that is
`formula-threshold`, and $\alpha q^k=1-e^{-x}-xe^{-x}$ becomes $\psi(x)=k$.
So $\xi_k$ is the value of $x$ at the threshold, the Poisson parameter of the
2-core calculation. I checked this algebra.

Smallest fix: replace the comment with that fact, in T287's own symbols. For
example: "$\xi_k$ is the value at $\alpha=\alpha_k$ of the parameter $x$ in
the 2-core description of the threshold, $x=k\alpha q^{k-1}$ with
$q=1-e^{-x}$, CITE{PittelSorkin}." If this is written up as the degree law of
the 2-core, check the wording against Pittel and Sorkin first. Otherwise just
delete the comment.

### 4. Noted only

- **Every row has the same comment**, "The associated satisfiability
  threshold is $\alpha_k$", linked to the row. That is ten short lines, and a
  link to the matching row is worth having, so leave it.
- **`complete-note` ends "matching the companion satisfiability-threshold
  table"**, which points at a table rather than naming it. T272's note gives
  its own range as "matching the companion graph-core table". So the reason
  for stopping at 12 is two steps removed, and nobody meets $\xi_{12}$ except
  through $\alpha_{12}$. It is harmless at ten rows. If finding 1(b) is
  chosen, the range needs rewriting anyway.
- **`formula-root` restates the Definition's equation with $\psi$ named.**
  That is fine: `rigour details` uses $\psi$, and this is where $\psi$ is
  defined.
- **Outside this table:** T272's rows (and T274's $\ell=1$ rows) hold
  $\xi_k$ as 12-digit numbers in entry comments. Once T287 is public, those
  comments could link `HREF{T287#k}` instead of repeating a shortened value.
  That is a change to those tables, not to this one.

Nothing I found is a rendering fault.
