**What I checked hardest, and found sound:**

* **It is one table.** `singularity` has 41 name-like values, but each names
  *what the polynomial is of*. `n` is an argument. The column is headed
  $\Delta_f(t)$, not `value`, and the `n` column reads `1`, `2`, `3`.
* **Rendering of the mathematics.** `h_*`, `\det`, `\mathbb{Z}[t]`,
  `\left(...\right)`, `\frac{n-c}{2}`, the `Φ` products and exponents like
  `\Phi_{2}(t)^{2}` all reach the page intact. No field has a `<` in its
  mathematics. `CITE{Milnor}` and `CITE{AGV}` resolve to [2] and [1]. The
  Poincaré link resolves (200). The rigour note folds after its first

* **The prose claims.** The formula for the eigenvalues gives, for $E_6$ at

  the stored rows. The T290 critique's points about the hyperbolic families,
  the modulus and an undefined $\mu$ have all been dealt with here from the
  start.

The table reads well, and the entry comments, each a normal form plus a

## Worth doing

### 1. Formulas and Comments share the key `suspension`, so the page numbers them `(1), (4)` and `(3), (4)`

Rendered:

> **Formulas**
> (1) If $f$ has weighted degree $1$ …
> (4) If $\mu$ is the Milnor number, then $\Delta_{f+u^2}(t)=(-1)^\mu\Delta_f(-t)$.
>
> **Comments**
> (3) $\mu$ is the Milnor number …
> (4) Increasing $n$ by one adds a square variable and suspends the singularity; every monodromy eigenvalue is multiplied by $-1$.

The labels are numbered by key, so the two `suspension` blocks get the same
number, and no block is numbered (2). The HTML also has
`id="suspension"` twice, so an address `#suspension` cannot say which block it
means. A reader sees two different "(4)"s and a missing "(2)".

The comment also says in words what the formula already states.

Fix: delete the `suspension` comment. The formula says it, and "every
eigenvalue is multiplied by $-1$" is what $\Delta_f(-t)$ means. If the words
are wanted, rename one of the two keys, for example `suspension-eigenvalues`.

### 2. "Middle homology" gives the wrong rows at $n=1$; it has to be reduced homology

Definition: "…on the middle homology of the Milnor fibre of
$f+x_{c+1}^2+\cdots+x_n^2$."

At $n=1$ the Milnor fibre of $x^{k+1}$ is $k+1$ points. Its middle homology
$H_0$ has rank $k+1$, and the monodromy permutes the points cyclically, so the
characteristic polynomial is $t^{k+1}-1$. The table stores
$(t^{k+1}-1)/(t-1)$: for $A_1$, $n=1$ it holds $t+1$, not $t^2-1$. That is the
reduced homology $\tilde H_0$. Someone building from the Definition as written
would get all twelve $A_k$, $n=1$ rows wrong. For $n\ge2$ the two agree.

Fix: "on the reduced middle homology $\tilde H_{n-1}$ of the Milnor fibre".

### 3. $\Phi_k$ is used on every row and defined nowhere, and the corpus holds it

Every entry comment ends like "$\Delta_f(t)=\Phi_{2}(t)^{2}\,\Phi_{4}(t)^{2}\,\Phi_{6}(t)\,\Phi_{12}(t)$".
Nothing on the page says that $\Phi_k$ is the $k$th cyclotomic polynomial, and
T95 is
[Cyclotomic polynomials](https://numberdb.org/Cyclotomic_polynomials)
(`Cyclotomic_polynomials`, slug read from `api/lookup`).

Fix: extend the `notation` comment: "…The degree of $\Delta_f(t)$ is $\mu$,
and $\Phi_k(t)$ is the $k$th HREF{Cyclotomic_polynomials}[cyclotomic
polynomial]."

### 4. The table does not point at the Alexander polynomials, which it equals on six rows

For an irreducible plane curve singularity the link is a knot, and $\Delta_f$
at $n=2$ is its Alexander polynomial (Milnor). The link of $x^p+y^q$ with $p,q$
coprime is the torus knot $T(p,q)$. T138 holds the Alexander polynomials of
the prime knots with at most ten crossings, and the stored polynomials are
identical:

| T291 row | value | T138 row |
|---|---|---|
| $A_2$, $n=2$ | $t^2-t+1$ | $3_1$ |
| $A_4$, $n=2$ | $t^4-t^3+t^2-t+1$ | $5_1$ |
| $A_6$, $n=2$ | $t^6-\cdots+1$ | $7_1$ |
| $A_8$, $n=2$ | $t^8-\cdots+1$ | $9_1$ |
| $E_6$, $n=2$ | $t^6-t^5+t^3-t+1$ | $8_{19}$ |
| $E_8$, $n=2$ | $t^8-t^7+t^5-t^4+t^3-t+1$ | $10_{124}$ |

A reader who arrives from a knot with $t^6-t^5+t^3-t+1$ finds $E_6$ here and
should be told why. This is the "different context is the product" case.

Fix: add a Similar tables entry,
`HREF{Alexander_polynomials_of_the_prime_knots_with_at_most_ten_crossings}[Alexander polynomials of the prime knots with at most ten crossings]`,
with the relation "for an irreducible plane curve singularity ($n=2$) the
monodromy polynomial is the Alexander polynomial of its link, a torus knot
$T(p,q)$ for $x^p+y^q$: $A_2$, $A_4$, $A_6$, $A_8$, $E_6$ and $E_8$ are the
knots $3_1$, $5_1$, $7_1$, $9_1$, $8_{19}$ and $10_{124}$". The slug was read
from `api/lookup`.

## Noted only

### 5. The same added variable goes by four names

The Definition adds $x_{c+1},\ldots,x_n$ to $f$ in $x,y,z$. The entry
comments add $y^2$ and $z^2$, and write the whole sum as $f$
("$f=x^{2}+y^2$"), while the Definition says $f$ is the normal form alone. The
suspension formula adds $u^2$. The `notation` comment takes the Milnor algebra
in $\mathbb{C}[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ of $f$. Read literally, that is
infinite-dimensional when $n>c$, because $\partial f$ does not involve the
added variables. The formula for the eigenvalues correctly uses
$x_1,\ldots,x_c$. Nobody will be misled for long, but the `notation` comment
is the one to fix: write $x_1,\ldots,x_c$ there, and say in the Definition
that $c$ is the corank, which is the word the `n` constraint uses.

### 6. `HREF{T290}` points at a draft

It renders as `href="T290"`, and `/T290` is 404 to a reader today. A T-number
is served directly, so the link will work once T290 is public. It only matters
if T291 is published first. Whoever reviews the pair should publish T290 first.

### 7. Two sentences that describe rather than state

* Formula (1) ends "…each with multiplicity. The product of $t$ minus these
  eigenvalues is $\Delta_f(t)$." That is a formula written out in words:
  $\Delta_f(t)=\prod_a\bigl(t-\exp(\ldots)\bigr)$ says it.
* `rigour details`: "Checks compare every row with Singular's `spectrum`…"
  does not say that the check was run on these rows or that it agreed. If it
  was, say "every row agreed with…". This is the same wording T290 was noted
  for.

`complete-note` also uses "exceptional" in two senses in one sentence ("the
exceptional simple singularities, and the parabolic and exceptional unimodal
singularities"). Writing "$E_6$, $E_7$, $E_8$" for the first would avoid that.

### 8. The Coxeter relation stops one step short, as in T290

T257 holds Poincaré polynomials, not exponents. A reader following the link
needs to know that the degrees $d_i$ read off there give the eigenvalues
$e^{2\pi i(d_i-1)/h}$. The relation is true as written.

Two further points are not faults. The $A_k$ rows at $n=3$ repeat the rows at
$n=1$, because suspending twice multiplies by $(-1)^{2\mu}$. They are true
rows that a reader holding a surface singularity wants, and 72 of the 87
values are distinct. `Tags` is only `polynomial`. With T290 there are now two
singularity tables, one short of the three the skill asks for before a new tag.
