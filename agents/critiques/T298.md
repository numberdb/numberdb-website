# Critique of T298, Values of the Rogers–Ramanujan continued fraction $R(e^{-\pi\sqrt r})$

Read on 2026-09-17, as a draft.

## How it was read

* **Rendering.** T298 is a private draft. `/T298` answers 404 even with
  the zeta3 bearer key, because the page route checks the site session. The
  SOCKS proxy refused every connection during this run, and numberdb.org
  answered curl directly. So I built the page through `/preview?table=...` in
  six pieces, each under 2 KB. Every section is covered, and all six answered
  200. MathJax 3 (`base`, `ams`) typeset all 27 `$...$` fragments without
  error. As a control, a fragment with an unclosed brace did raise an error.
  Every `CITE{}` and `HREF{}` resolves, and the formula references render as
  (1), (2) and (3).
* **Document.** `GET /api/table?id=T298` with the key. It has 100 entries,
  $r=1,\dots,100$.
* **Audit.** `GET /api/table/T298/audit` answered `"findings": [], "clean":
  true`. I agree that nothing it checks for is present. It misses findings 1
  to 4 below, and none of them is the kind of thing it checks.
* **Mathematics that a reader could be misled by**, checked in Sage from the
  stored values:
  * Formula (2) gives $j(i\sqrt r/2)$ exactly at $r=1,2,4,8,12,16,28$:
    287496, 8000, 1728, 8000, 54000, 287496 and 16581375.
  * Formula (3) holds to $10^{-90}$ at $r=1,2,5,25$.
  * Row 4 equals $\sqrt{(5+\sqrt5)/2}-\varphi$.
  * Row 20 equals $\sqrt5/\bigl(1+(5^{3/4}(\varphi-1)^{5/2}-1)^{1/5}\bigr)-\varphi$.

  Both closed forms hold to the 90 digits the check carried.

It is one table, with one parameter, and it holds one object. The title,
keywords and number header all agree, and the page renders cleanly. The
findings are about two sentences of prose, and one of them sends a reader to
the wrong row of another table.

## Worth doing

### 1. Comment (1) names a CM point that is not the one Formula (2) uses. Medium.

The page reads:

> (1) The CM point is $\tau=i\sqrt r$, so the family nome is
> $q=e^{\pi i\tau}=e^{-\pi\sqrt r}$. The order is $\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-r}]$, of
> discriminant $-4r$.

Formula (2) then writes $q=e^{2\pi iz}$ with $z=i\sqrt r/2$ and gives $j(z)$.
Both are true. But $R$ is a modular function of $z$, the variable in
$q=e^{2\pi iz}$, so the point of the upper half plane that this row belongs
to is $z$, not $\tau$. Its order is not $\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-r}]$ for odd $r$.

This matters because the page invites the reader to follow it through.
`Similar tables` says Formula (2) "relates these values to singular values of
the modular $j$-invariant" and links T83. A reader who takes comment (1) at
its word looks at $r=1$, expects $\tau=i$ and $j=1728$ (T83 row $-4$), and
gets 287496 (row $-16$). The row that gives 1728 is $r=4$. The phrase "the
family nome" adds a second problem, because it is the only place on the page
that uses the $e^{\pi i\tau}$ convention.

**Fix:** replace comment (1) with a sentence in the variable the formulas use:
"With $z=i\sqrt r/2$ the row is $R$ at $q=e^{2\pi iz}$. This is the nome
$e^{\pi i\tau}$ of $\tau=i\sqrt r$, which is the convention of
HREF{T295}[the singular moduli $k_r$]." Drop the sentence about the order. If
it stays, it has to be the order of $z$, which depends on $r$ modulo 4, and
that is more than a comment needs.

### 2. The most-quoted value in the table is not identified. Medium.

A reader holding 0.2840790438… has almost certainly met
$R(e^{-2\pi})=\sqrt{(5+\sqrt5)/2}-\varphi$, the evaluation Ramanujan sent to
Hardy. That reader lands on "4: 0.28407…" and is not told that this is the
number. The same holds for row 20, which is
$R(e^{-2\pi\sqrt5})=\sqrt5/\bigl(1+(5^{3/4}(\varphi-1)^{5/2}-1)^{1/5}\bigr)-\varphi$.
Both closed forms match the stored values (see above).

**Fix:** add an entry comment on rows 4 and 20 giving the closed form. Two
comments, not a hundred.

### 3. The parameter reads "positive integer (positive integer)". Small.

It renders as `$r$ — positive integer (positive integer)`: the title and the
constraint say the same thing. The title should say what $r$ *is*.

**Fix:** set the title to "the $r$ in the nome $e^{-\pi\sqrt r}$" and keep
the constraint.

### 4. Two sentences that do not quite say what they mean. Small.

* Definition: "The row stores the Rogers–Ramanujan continued fraction
  $R(q)=\dots$". A definition says what the object is, and "the row" is a word
  about the website. It also makes the sentence claim that a row stores a
  function.
  **Fix:** "The Rogers–Ramanujan continued fraction is $R(q)=\dots$, with
  $q^{1/5}$ the positive real fifth root. Each entry is $R(q)$ at
  $q=e^{-\pi\sqrt r}$ for a positive integer $r$."
* Comment (2): "For these rows $0<q<1$, so the continued fraction converges to
  its value inside the unit disk." The phrase "its value inside the unit disk"
  reads as though the *value* lies in the disk, when what lies in the disk is
  $q$.
  **Fix:** "The continued fraction converges for $|q|<1$, so at every
  $q=e^{-\pi\sqrt r}$." Deleting the comment would also be fine.

## Noted only because I noticed

5. **The T83 relation could name its rows.** Through Formula (2), rows
   $r=4,8,12,16,28$ give T83's $\Delta=-4,-8,-12,-16,-28$. Rows 1 and 2 give
   $-16$ and $-8$ again. Saying so would give the relation content and would
   also make finding 1 impossible to repeat.
6. **The T297 relation is weak.** It says "both are Ramanujan modular
   functions evaluated at the same real nomes". That is true, but it would fit
   any two tables in this batch.
7. **`rigour details` says "an explicit positive tail bound".** It is not
   clear what "positive" modifies. "An explicit bound on the tail of the
   product" says what is meant.
8. **Three of the four sibling links point at drafts** (T295, T296, T297).
   They resolve for the author now, but will 404 for readers unless the four
   tables are published together.

## Summary

The table reads well. The definition defines, the single parameter describes
the family, and the range is in `complete-note` with a reason. The page
renders without a fault. Worth doing, in order:

1. Restate comment (1) in the formulas' variable $z=i\sqrt r/2$, so that
   Formula (2) and T83 line up with the rows.
2. Add closed-form comments on rows 4 and 20.
3. Fix the parameter title, and the two sentences in finding 4.

Findings 5 to 8 can be left alone.
