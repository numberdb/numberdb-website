## Findings, ranked

### 1. The values a reader most often arrives holding have closed forms, and the page gives none of them

A reader who computed $\zeta'(-1)$ holds $-0.16542114\ldots$. On the first
screen they find it as the third row, "$-1$, $\zeta'(s)$", with no comment.
It is $\tfrac1{12}-\log A$, with $A$ the Glaisher–Kinkelin constant, and that
identity is why the number turns up in Barnes $G$, in regularised
determinants and in Casimir energies. The same holds for three more rows:

| row | stored | closed form | checked, difference below |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\zeta'(-1)$ | $-0.1654211437\ldots$ | $\tfrac1{12}-\log A$ | $10^{-100}$ |
| $\zeta'(2)$ | $-0.9375482543\ldots$ | $\tfrac{\pi^2}6(\gamma+\log 2\pi-12\log A)$ | $10^{-101}$ |
| $\zeta'(2)/\zeta(2)$ | $-0.5699609930\ldots$ | $\gamma+\log 2\pi-12\log A$ | (the row above divided by $\pi^2/6$) |
| $\zeta'(\tfrac12)/\zeta(\tfrac12)$ | $2.6860917096\ldots$ | $\tfrac12(\tfrac\pi2+\gamma+\log 8\pi)$ | $10^{-100}$ |

The source for the first two is DLMF 5.17.7, which gives
$\log A=\tfrac{\gamma+\log 2\pi}{12}-\tfrac{\zeta'(2)}{2\pi^2}=\tfrac1{12}-\zeta'(-1)$.
The batch proposal knew both of them. Its "why a reader might meet one" says
exactly that $\zeta'(-1)$ is $\tfrac1{12}-\log A$. It did not reach the page.

The row at $s=2$ has a comment, and it spends it repeating formula (2):
"The negative of this value is $\sum_{n\geq1}\Lambda(n)/n^2$."

Behind this sits a whole column. For $k\geq1$,
$\zeta'(-k)=\frac{B_{k+1}}{k+1}H_k-\log A_k$, where the $A_k$ are the Bendersky
constants. That is T227's own definition, read backwards. Checked for
$k=1,\ldots,20$ against T227's stored $\log A_k$, every difference is below
$10^{-98}$ relative. At even $k$ the rows are the same digits with opposite
sign. T227 is a draft from the same batch and the same owner, and it is the
table most tightly bound to these rows, yet it is not in Similar tables. Two
drafts may link to each other, but whoever publishes one first must publish
the other with it, or the audit will flag a link to a draft.

Smallest fix, in four parts:

- Add entry comments: on $\zeta'(-1)$, "$\frac1{12}-\log A$, where $A$ is
  HREF{T227#1,A}[the Glaisher–Kinkelin constant] CITE{DLMFBarnes}."; on
  $\zeta'(2)$, "$\frac{\pi^2}{6}(\gamma+\log 2\pi-12\log A)$ CITE{DLMFBarnes}.";
  on $\zeta'(1/2)/\zeta(1/2)$, "$\frac12(\frac\pi2+\gamma+\log 8\pi)$.".
- Replace the comment at $\zeta'(2)/\zeta(2)$ with
  "$\gamma+\log(2\pi)-12\log A$.".
- Add a formula: "For $k\geq1$, $\zeta'(-k)=\frac{B_{k+1}}{k+1}H_k-\log A_k$,
  where $H_k=\sum_{j=1}^k 1/j$ and $A_k$ are the HREF{T227}[Bendersky
  constants]."
- Add a Similar-tables entry for T227 with that relation, and a Link
  `DLMFBarnes`, titled "DLMF 5.17: Barnes' G-Function (Double Gamma
  Function)", at <https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.17>.

`HREF{T227...}` resolves by T-number. Read the entry key off T227 rather than
trusting mine. Its `Numbers` are keyed `k` and then `A` or `logA`, so the row
for $A$ is `1,A`.

Worth doing. It is the difference between a reader learning the name of their
number and learning only that someone else has computed it too.

### 2. Formula (2) cites a DLMF section that does not contain it, and both DLMF links misname their sections

A reader sees "For $\Re(s)>1$, $-\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s)=\sum\Lambda(n)n^{-s}$ [1]"
and follows [1] to <https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.2>. That section has
$\zeta'(s)=-\sum(\ln n)n^{-s}$ (25.2.6) but no $\Lambda$ anywhere. It sends
Dirichlet series to §27.4, where the formula is 27.4.12. The link titles are
also not DLMF's. §25.2 is "Definition and Expansions", not "Definitions", and
§25.6 is "Integer Arguments", not "Special Values".

Smallest fix: add a Link `DLMFDirichlet`, titled "DLMF 27.4: Euler Products
and Dirichlet Series", at <https://dlmf.nist.gov/27.4>, and cite it from
formula (2). Retitle the other two "DLMF 25.2: Definition and Expansions"
and "DLMF 25.6: Integer Arguments". `DLMFZeta` then earns its place as the
citation for the definition in finding 3.

Worth doing: it is small and it is certainly wrong. A lesson proposal is
filed, since a contributor on a laptop would make the same mistake.

### 3. The Definition does not parse as a sentence, and it names the parameter by allusion

A reader sees: "Listed are $\zeta'(s)=\frac{d}{ds}\zeta(s)$ for the Riemann
zeta function and the logarithmic derivative $\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s)$ at rational
arguments $s$ where the chosen quantity is finite."

The reader has to guess three things. First, whether "for the Riemann zeta
function" belongs to $\zeta'$ alone or to both quantities. Second, whether
"at rational arguments" belongs to both quantities or only to the logarithmic
derivative. Third, what "the chosen quantity" is: nothing before it says a
quantity is chosen, because that is the parameter `quantity` speaking through
the prose. "Where ... is finite" is a remark about the range, and the
constraint and comment (2) already make it. Formula (1),
$\frac{d}{ds}\log\zeta(s)=\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s)$, is in fact the definition of the
second quantity, so it belongs here rather than among the identities.

Since `6dcbaeb` the Definition also carries nothing that explains "the
Riemann zeta function". The link was removed for the audit and no `CITE` was
added. Two earlier lessons already settle this: a Definition cites and does
not link.

Smallest fix, replacing the Definition and deleting formula (1):

> $\zeta(s)$ is the Riemann zeta function, $\sum_{n\geq1}n^{-s}$ for
> $\Re(s)>1$ continued meromorphically to $s\neq1$ CITE{DLMFZeta}. The table
> holds its derivative $\zeta'(s)$ and its logarithmic derivative
> $\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s)$ at rational $s$.

Worth doing. This is the first prose a reader meets under the numbers.

### 4. The title names half of what the table holds

230 of the 470 rows are $\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s)$, and the title says
"derivative". Search is covered, because `Keywords` carries "logarithmic
derivative of the Riemann zeta function" at the title's weight. A reader
scanning `/tables`, or landing on the page from the $\Lambda$-series value
$-0.5699\ldots$, still reads a heading that describes the other quantity.

Smallest fix: "Values of the derivative $\zeta'(s)$ and logarithmic
derivative $\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s)$ of the Riemann zeta function at rational
numbers".

Whether these are one table at all is a decision, and the batch proposal made
it on purpose. It called $\zeta'/\zeta$ "a normalisation of the same
derivative" and recorded the opposing argument. For whoever reviews: the
skill's own test points the other way. "A shared *argument* is not that",
and its examples of one object in two conventions ($E_1$ and $\mathrm{Ei}$,
$\beta$ and $e^\beta$) are rescalings of one function, not a quotient by
another function. T34 already treats the logarithmic derivative as a
separately named function. I would retitle and leave the split to the
reviewer. Retitling is worth doing either way.

### 5. Comment (1) repeats formula (2), and $\Lambda$ is never defined

A reader sees "The logarithmic derivative is stored with its own sign. Its
negative is the von Mangoldt Dirichlet series
$-\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s)=\sum_{n\geq1}\Lambda(n)n^{-s}$ for $\Re(s)>1$." The
second sentence is formula (2) word for word. "Stored with its own sign"
makes the reader work out that it means "not negated". $\Lambda(n)$ appears
three times in the table, in comment (1), formula (2) and the $s=2$ row, and
nothing says what it is.

Smallest fix, as comment (1): "$\Lambda(n)$ is the von Mangoldt function,
$\log p$ when $n$ is a power of a prime $p$ and $0$ otherwise. The table holds
$\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s)$ itself, not its negative, which is the Dirichlet series
in CITE{formula-von-mangoldt}." Worth doing; it is one field.

### 7. Nothing tells a reader how the row at $1-s$ relates to the row at $s$

Half the arguments are negative, and a reader holding a value at $s<0$ is
most helped by its relation to the positive side. Two identities give that,
and both hold on the stored rows:

- $\dfrac{\zeta'(1-s)}{\zeta(1-s)}=\log(2\pi)+\dfrac\pi2\tan\dfrac{\pi s}2-\psi(s)-\dfrac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}$
  for $s\notin\mathbb Z$. This is the logarithmic derivative of DLMF 25.4.1.
  I checked it at $s=\tfrac13,\tfrac54,\tfrac72,-\tfrac32,\tfrac14,-\tfrac{19}3$
  using both stored rows, and every difference is below $3\cdot10^{-100}$.
- $\zeta'(2n)=\dfrac{(-1)^{n+1}(2\pi)^{2n}}{2(2n)!}\bigl(2n\,\zeta'(1-2n)-(\psi(2n)-\log 2\pi)B_{2n}\bigr)$,
  which is DLMF 25.6.15. I checked it for $n=1,\ldots,10$ using both stored
  rows, and every difference is below $10^{-99}$.

Smallest fix: add both under Formulas, citing a new Link for DLMF 25.4 and
the existing `DLMFSpecial`. Worth doing if the table is reopened for findings
1 to 3. Without them the page is correct, only less useful.

## Noted only

* **The complete-note does not say why this range.** It says what is present
  ($b\leq4$, $|s|\leq20$) but not why, which the skill asks of that sentence.
  The proposal's reason ("halves, thirds and quarters are where the moment
  conjectures and Hurwitz decompositions land") did not reach the page, and I
  doubt it covers rows such as $\zeta'(-\tfrac{59}4)$ or $\zeta'(\tfrac{47}3)$.
  The reviewer should know the range was chosen by a bound.
* **Formula (4) cites §25.6.** There the identity appears only as the $k=1$
  case of 25.6.13, with $c$ taken from 25.4.6. "DLMF 25.6.13 with $k=1$"
  would spare a reader the specialisation. The identity itself is right, and
  I checked the specialisation by hand.
* **The constraint** "for $\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s)$, also $\zeta(s)\neq0$" is, for
  rational $s$, the same as "$s\neq-2,-4,\ldots$", which a reader can check
  without knowing where $\zeta$ vanishes.
* **$\zeta'(\tfrac12)$ is row 71**, after every integer argument, because rows
  are ordered by denominator first. The anchor and search reach it. Ordering
  by $\max(|a|,b)$ would put it eighth, but it is not worth regenerating for.
* **Similar tables, T94:** "$\zeta(s)$ is the Hurwitz zeta function at
  parameter $1$" would read better as "at $x=1$", since T94's parameters are
  $x$ and $s$.
* **T93, the Barnes $G$ values,** states $G(1/2)=2^{1/24}e^{\frac32\zeta'(-1)}\pi^{-1/4}$
  as a formula. It is a fair Similar-tables candidate once finding 1 names
  $\zeta'(-1)$.
* **Rigour details** say the entries were "checked against the values of
  $\zeta(s)$" in T14. T14 holds 174 of these 240 arguments, and that check
  bears only on the quotient. The sentence is not false.

  `number` strings in exponent form at $s=20$ render as written. The column
  header "value" is accurate for both quantities.
* **Parameters versus run.** The constraint describes the family ($s\neq1$,
  and the zeros excluded for the quotient). The range lives only in the
  complete-note, which finishes its sentence.
* **The mathematics that is on the page.** Formulas (3) and (4) and the
  comments $-\frac12\log 2\pi$, $\log 2\pi$, $-\zeta(3)/(4\pi^2)$ and

  Comment (2) is right that the logarithmic derivative has simple poles at the
  trivial zeros while $\zeta'$ does not.
