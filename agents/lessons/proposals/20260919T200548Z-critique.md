# Lessons proposed by the T287 growth critique, 2026-09-19

## An absolute-tolerance cross-check stops checking anything where the family converges

What happened: T287 holds $\xi_k$, the root of $\psi(\xi)=k$ with
$\psi(x)=x+x^2/(e^x-1-x)$, for $3\leq k\leq12$, and its generator verifies
every row against an independent mpmath solve:

    tolerance = RBF("1e-95")
    ...
    if not (abs(xi - independent) < tolerance):
        raise ArithmeticError("mpmath root comparison failed for k=%d" % k)

That is a good check inside the published range and it is worthless outside it,
because the values converge: $k-\xi_k=\xi_k^2/(e^{\xi_k}-1-\xi_k)\approx
k^2e^{-k}$. Once the gap is smaller than the tolerance, both the ball and the
mpmath root round to the integer $k$ and the comparison passes without
comparing anything -- a second implementation of the same equation, a
mis-transcribed equation, or `return RBF(k)` would all pass alike. Measured
with the table's own generator: at $k=229$ the gap is $1.8\cdot10^{-95}$ and
the check still has teeth; at $k=250$ it is $1.7\cdot10^{-104}$ and the check is
met by the convergence alone. The same arithmetic makes the table itself
pointless there -- at $k=235$ the 100 stored digits *are* the integer -- so in
practice the two failures arrive together, and the dangerous case is the range
in between, where the row still carries information and the check no longer
confirms it.

The general shape: a check written as `abs(mine - theirs) < eps` tests the
digits a reader would use only while the quantity's *own* variation exceeds
`eps`. For a family tending to a limit -- an integer, $1$, a closed form -- the
variation is the deviation from that limit, and it shrinks with the parameter
while `eps` does not.

What the skill says now: section 7 requires the rigour to be stated and
`rigour details` to say how the digits were obtained, and the corpus's practice
is to compare against a second implementation. Nothing says how to choose the
tolerance of that comparison, and nothing warns that a fixed absolute tolerance
degrades along the very axis a table grows on.

What it should say: compare with a tolerance scaled to what the row actually
says. Where a family tends to a limit, check the deviation from the limit
($k-\xi_k$ against $\xi_k^2/(e^{\xi_k}-1-\xi_k)$ here) rather than the value,
or make the tolerance relative to that deviation. Then the check still fails
when it should at the far end of the range, and it tells you where the range
should stop: the parameter at which the deviation falls below the stored
precision is the parameter at which the table has nothing left to store.

Evidence: 2026-09-19, `agents/critiques/T287-growth.md`.
`generators/xorsat-threshold-equation-roots/generate.py:run_integrity_checks`.
Measured under `agents/sage.sh` with that generator's own `root` and
`_mpmath_root`: $|{\rm ball}-{\rm mpmath}|\approx10^{-108}$ at every $k$ tried,
while $k-\xi_k$ falls from $8.9\cdot10^{-4}$ at $k=12$ to $1.7\cdot10^{-104}$ at
$k=250$, crossing the $10^{-95}$ tolerance near $k=229$; rendering the rows from
$k=225$ to $244$ shows $k=234$ as the last one whose 100 digits differ from the
integer.
