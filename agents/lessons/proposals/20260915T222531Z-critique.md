## A real ball's `psi()` is the digamma function only; the order goes to a complex ball

What happened: while checking the reflection formula for the polygamma table
T250, `RealBallField(400)(QQ(1)/4).psi(1)` raised `TypeError: psi() takes
exactly 0 positional arguments (1 given)`. A real ball's `psi` computes
$\psi$ and nothing else. `ComplexBallField(prec)(x).psi(n)` computes
$\psi^{(n)}$, and that is what T250's generator and its Programs snippet use.
To get a real value, take `.real()` of the result after checking that the
imaginary part contains zero.

What the skill says now: nothing about `psi`. Section 7 lists what arb
implements, and all of it through ball elements.

What it should say: in the list of arb functions in section 7, "`psi(n)`
(polygamma of order $n$) on a `ComplexBallField` element; the real ball's
`psi()` takes no order".

Evidence: 2026-09-15, T250 critique. `/tmp/crit250/refl.py` under
`agents/sage.sh` gave the TypeError with `RealBallField`. With
`ComplexBallField(400)` it printed `n=1 [+/- 1.23e-118]` for
$\psi'(1/4)+\psi'(3/4)-\pi^2/\sin^2(\pi/4)$.
