## A formula copied from a source keeps the source's range condition

What happened: T270 (Krawtchouk polynomials in the Askey normalisation)
copied DLMF 18.23.3, the generating function
$\sum_n\binom Nn K_n(x;p,N)t^n=(1-\frac{1-p}{p}t)^x(1+t)^{N-x}$, but left out
DLMF's condition "$x=0,1,\ldots,N$". A comment in the same table says the
entries are polynomials in the indeterminate $x$, so a reader takes the
formula as an identity in $x$, and it is false there. At $N=1$, $p=\tfrac12$,
$x=2$, $t=\tfrac25$ the left side is $-\tfrac15$ and the right side is
$\tfrac9{35}$, because the right side is not even a polynomial in $t$. The
build had "checked against the generating function", and the check passed
because it only evaluated at the support points where the identity holds.
`audit_table` cannot see this.

What the skill says now: nothing about the conditions on a formula. §10
says to check new values against identities.

What it should say: under **Formulas** in §4, a formula states the range it
holds on, copied with the formula from its source. For a table of
polynomials, an identity that holds only at integer points of a support has
to say so, because the table invites the reader to treat the variable as
free. When a formula is checked, test it at a point where the condition
fails too, and confirm that it does fail there. That is the control from
§10, and it shows that the condition is doing something.

Evidence: `agents/critiques/T270.md`, finding 1. The identity was evaluated
in Python `Fraction`s against all 760 stored rows at $x\in\{0,1,2\}$. It
failed at $x=2$, $N=1$ for each of the five $p$ and held everywhere else.
