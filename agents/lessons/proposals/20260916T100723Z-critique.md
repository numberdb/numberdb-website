## A relation stated in a comment needs the same check as a formula

What happened: the T268 draft (Hahn polynomials) checked every entry against
each identity in `Formulas`: orthogonality, the forward difference, $Q_n(0)=1$
and the Jacobi limit. All of them hold. The two mathematical claims that were
wrong were written in `Comments`, as prose, and nothing checked them. One
said that swapping $(\alpha,\beta)$ is the same as replacing $x$ with $N-x$.
That drops the factor $(-1)^n(\beta+1)_n/(\alpha+1)_n$, and it cannot be right
for a family normalised by $Q_n(0)=1$. The other said the $\alpha=\beta=0$
rows are analogues of the Chebyshev polynomials. By the table's own limit
they tend to the Legendre polynomials; Chebyshev is $\alpha=\beta=\mp\frac12$.
A second, smaller slip: the comment called Wikipedia's $-N+1$ convention
"singular". It is an index shift, and Wikipedia's $Q_n(\cdot;N)$ is this
table's $Q_n(\cdot;N-1)$.

What the skill says now: "Check new values against something independent"
and "Verify a claim before writing it into a table". Both are read as being
about values and tags. Nothing says that a relation written in prose is a
claim in the same sense as a formula.

What it should say: any relation between rows, and any limit or special case
a comment names, is a formula written in words. Check it on the stored rows
the way the formulas are checked, and preferably move it into `Formulas`.
Symmetries of a normalised family need extra care, because the normalisation
seldom survives the symmetry. When a comment compares the table with another
source's convention, give the map between the two ($N\mapsto N-1$). Do not
describe the other convention as wrong.

Evidence: 2026-09-16, T268 critique (`agents/critiques/T268.md`). The stored
rows give $Q_n(9-x;1,2,9)/Q_n(x;2,1,9)=-3/2,\,2,\,-5/2,\,3$ for $n=1..4$. At
$N=20000$, $Q_3(0.2N;0,0,N)=-0.36014$ against $P_3(0.6)=-0.36$ and
$T_3(0.6)=-0.936$.
