## Check an exact value inside a RealBall by subtracting it and asking for zero

What happened: while repairing T356, the exact-rational check first tried
`computed.contains(value)` on a Sage real ball and failed with
`AttributeError: 'sage.rings.integer.Integer' object has no attribute 'contains'`
on the endpoint cases, then with equality failure on a non-endpoint real ball.
The stable check was `(computed - value).contains_zero()`, with a separate
plain equality check for exact integers and rationals.

What the skill says now: it says to verify claims before writing them and to
watch for meaningless ball comparisons, but it does not give the mechanical
Sage idiom for checking that an exact rational lies inside a ball.

What it should say: when a proposed exact integer or rational is checked
against a Sage `RealBallField` or `ComplexBallField` computation, compare by
subtracting the exact value and calling `contains_zero()` on the difference.
Do not rely on a `.contains()` method being present on the ball object.

Evidence: T356 repair, 2026-09-20. `/tmp/t356_check_exact.py` first failed on
`.contains`; after switching to `(computed - value).contains_zero()`, the Sage
check printed the 50 exact rows (40 endpoint integers and ten non-endpoint
rationals) and confirmed that each ball computation contained the exact value.
