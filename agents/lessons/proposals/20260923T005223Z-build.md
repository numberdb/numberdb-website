## A Definition can trip both sides of the table-link audit

What happened: T425's Definition said that $K(m)$ is the complete elliptic
integral of the first kind. The audit reported: "Definition names Complete
elliptic integral of the first kind and does not link it." Adding the
suggested `HREF{Complete_elliptic_integral_of_the_first_kind_K}` made the next
audit report: "Definition links to another table; a cross-reference belongs in
Similar tables or a comment." The clean fix was to keep the Definition free of
the related table's title, write "Legendre's $K$" there, and put the explicit
NumberDB link in Comments and Similar tables.

What the skill says now: it says to link the first mention of a thing, and it
also says a Definition should not link to another table.

What it should say: when the first mention of a related table's exact title
would be in the Definition, either move that explanatory sentence to Comments
or use notation in the Definition and link the related table in Comments or
Similar tables. Do not satisfy the "names another table without linking it"
finding by putting an `HREF` in the Definition, because the audit refuses that
too.

Evidence: `/tmp/audit_t425.py` on 2026-09-23 first reported the missing link
for T425, then after the link was added reported that the Definition should
not link to another table. The final audit was clean after the Definition was
changed to "where $K$ is Legendre's $K$".
