## Do not put bracket notation inside an `HREF[...]` caption
What happened: the T223 repair tried to link the entry $[3,5,3]$ as
`HREF{#3,[3,5,3]}[$[3,5,3]$]`. The preview renderer made the link text
`$[3,5,3` and left `$]` outside it, because the first `]` in the Coxeter
symbol ended the caption. The target itself was not the problem; the caption
syntax cannot contain an unescaped square bracket.

What the skill says now: it says to use `HREF{slug}[caption]` and to read
entry keys out of `Numbers`, but it does not warn that the caption delimiter
is also ordinary mathematical notation.

What it should say: do not put square-bracket notation inside an `HREF[...]`
caption. If the name to link is itself bracketed, link nearby words or leave
the symbol unlinked; check the rendered preview for the final caption, not
only for the target.

Evidence: T223, 2026-09-13. `/tmp/T223-preview-comments.html` rendered the
bad caption as `<a ...>$[3,5,3</a>$]`; removing the internal HREF made the
preview render cleanly.
