## Give `repeats` links a caption
What happened: T439 had `Data properties: repeats: HREF{Diagonal_Ramsey_numbers}`. The repeat relation was recorded, but the page printed the slug `Diagonal_Ramsey_numbers` where a reader should see the table name.

What the skill says now: its example is `repeats: HREF{Other_table}`.

What it should say: write `repeats` as a normal internal link with a caption, for example `HREF{Other_table}[Other table]`, so the page prints the reader-facing table name rather than the slug.

Evidence: T439 repair, 2026-09-23. Changing `repeats` to `HREF{Diagonal_Ramsey_numbers}[Diagonal Ramsey numbers]` kept `/api/table/T439/audit` clean.

## Parameter letters stay bound in provenance fields
What happened: T439's Parameters define `$k_1,\ldots,k_r$` as the whole clique-size tuple, but `rigour details` and `complete-note` reused `$k$` and `$l$` for the two entries of a two-colour tuple.

What the skill says now: it says to define notation where it is used and not to name a parameter by its key.

What it should say: once a display symbol is bound in `Parameters`, do not reuse that symbol for another role anywhere on the page, including `rigour details` and `complete-note`. Rename source letters to the table's symbols before writing provenance prose.

Evidence: T439 repair, 2026-09-23. Rewriting the provenance fields as `$R(k_1,k_2)$ with $3\leq k_1\leq7$ and $k_1\leq k_2\leq13$` removed the collision, and `/api/table/T439/audit` stayed clean.
