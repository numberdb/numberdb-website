## A reference identifier of the right shape can still name no paper, or the wrong one

What happened: T223 cites Kellerhals, "The minimal growth rate of cocompact
Coxeter groups in hyperbolic 3-space", Canad. J. Math. 66 (2014), with
`doi: 10.4153/CJM-2012-037-8` and `arxiv: 0912.1384` (unquoted, so stored as
`912.1384`). Both links on the page answer 404. The lost leading zero is
already a proposal (see "An arXiv identifier with a leading zero must be
quoted in YAML" in `PROPOSALS.md`). Here, though, restoring the zero would not have helped:
arXiv:0912.1384 is a paper on phonons in carbon nanotubes, and the DOI is
not registered at all. `doi.org/api/handles/10.4153/CJM-2012-037-8` answers
`{"responseCode":100}`, while a control DOI answers `responseCode 1` with a
URL. A Crossref bibliographic search on the title gave the real record in
one call: DOI 10.4153/CJM-2012-062-3, authors Kellerhals **and Kolpakov**.
So the bib had also dropped a co-author. arXiv's author search lists no
preprint of the paper.

What the skill says now: the References row says to put `arxiv`, `doi`,
`zbl` and `mr` in their own fields so they render as links. Nothing says to
check that an identifier leads to the paper cited.

What it should say: an identifier is a claim, like a value. Before it goes
in, resolve it and compare titles. Run `curl
https://doi.org/api/handles/<doi>`: `responseCode 1` means registered and
`100` means not. Read `https://arxiv.org/abs/<id>` for its title. When
either fails, `api.crossref.org/works?query.bibliographic=<author title>`
returns the DOI, the full author list, volume and pages. A malformed-shape
check catches a dropped zero, but not an identifier that was never right.

Evidence: 2026-09-13. `curl https://doi.org/api/handles/10.4153/CJM-2012-037-8`
returns `{"responseCode":100,...}`, and the same for `10.4153/CJM-2012-062-3`
returns `responseCode 1` with a cambridge.org URL. `https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.1384`
has the title "Resonant Coherent Phonon Generation in Single-Walled Carbon
Nanotubes through Near-Band-Edge Excitation". The Crossref query for the
title returns authors `['Kellerhals', 'Kolpakov']`.
