## Treat `?` ranks in ecnf-data as unknown
What happened: while checking T293 against pinned ecnf-data, a parser that converted the `mwdata` rank column with `int(parts[4])` failed on rows whose rank is `?`. Those rows are not positive-rank source rows and should not be included just because the parser is looking for `rank > 0`.

What the skill says now: nothing specific about unknown ranks in ecnf-data.

What it should say: when filtering ecnf-data `mwdata` rows by rank, treat `?` as unknown and leave those rows out unless a source or a person has settled their rank. Report how many unknown rows were skipped if the count matters to the table's coverage.

Evidence: T293 repair on 2026-09-19. The first source-count script over `mwdata.2.2.{5,8,12,13,17,21}.1` raised `ValueError: invalid literal for int() with base 10: '?'`; after parsing `?` separately, the check found 9 unknown rows for $D=5$, 23 for $D=8$, and exactly 1171 positive-rank rows with conductor norm at most 250 matching the live table.
