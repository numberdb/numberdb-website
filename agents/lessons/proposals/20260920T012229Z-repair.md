## Keep cross-table references out of Definition
What happened: while repairing T346, a definition sentence was changed to link to the companion division-polynomial table for the curve convention. The table write succeeded, but `GET /api/table/T346/audit` then reported: `Definition links to another table; a cross-reference belongs in Similar tables or a comment`.

What the skill says now: it says to use `HREF{slug}` for tables in prose and to let the audit confirm links, but it does not say that the `Definition` field should not contain cross-table references.

What it should say: keep the `Definition` self-contained and free of `HREF{}` cross-references. Put cross-table context in `Similar tables`, `Comments`, or another prose section, then audit the result.

Evidence: T346 repair, 2026-09-20. Revision `06ef253bdb87864fc0eef718473e928cd856ea70c8611d346b9408a4ec245338` introduced the definition link and `/api/table/T346/audit` returned the finding above; revision `b7eb49c62f89e4fa083b095fef738bc52fc3628237290457db4f360e3079b925` moved the link to `comment-model`, after which the audit returned `clean: true`.
