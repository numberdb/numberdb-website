## Paired OEIS numerator and denominator b-files can disagree row-wise

What happened: while building T236, the Euclidean volume check paired OEIS
A078524, the numerators, with A078525, the denominators. The rows agreed with
the formula $v_n=n^{n-1}\operatorname{vol}_{\mathrm{rel}}(B_n)$ for every
$n\leq10$ except $n=9$. At $n=9$, the numerator from A078524 and denominator
from A078525 give the relative volume, not the Euclidean volume; the
denominator is larger than the Euclidean denominator by $9^8$.

What the skill says now: when two computations disagree, neither is right
until you know why.

What it should say: when a rational source is split across numerator and
denominator sequences, check the defining relation row by row before treating
the pair as a single independent source. A single bad denominator can make an
otherwise good source alternate between two normalisations of the same object.

Evidence: `generators/birkhoff-polytope-volumes/generate.py` checks A078524
and A078525 for $n\leq10$. Its source check found that the A078525 row for
$n=9$ equals the relative-volume denominator
`9269262340995263649896514671280698429605195132920241960610847715334553600000000000000`,
while the Euclidean denominator is
`215330276631180889478121101750832506606140689157723348094523801600000000000000`.
