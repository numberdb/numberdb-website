## Do not redirect a published table to a new draft during a split

What happened: splitting draft T239 created draft T248 for the
matching-generating polynomial. T240, a published independence-polynomial
table, had pointed at the old matching-polynomial slug. Replacing that link
with `HREF{T248}` made `GET /api/table/T240/audit` report
`HREF{T248} points at a draft, which answers 404 to everybody; a published
table must not link to one`. Removing the table link and leaving the
line-graph identity as plain prose made T240's audit clean again.

What the skill says now: it says a draft is invisible, answers no search, and
may be linked from the split table's `Similar tables`, but it does not spell
out what to do when a published neighbour points at the old slug of the draft
being split.

What it should say: when a split creates a new draft, do not redirect a
published table to that draft yet. Either leave the relation as plain prose
without `HREF{...}`, or wait until the new table is published and then add the
cross-reference. Draft-to-draft links are fine inside the split pair because
reviewers can see both; published-to-draft links are broken for everyone else.

Evidence: 2026-09-15 split of T239/T248. After changing T240's link to
`HREF{T248}`, `GET /api/table/T240/audit` returned one finding: `HREF{T248}
points at a draft, which answers 404 to everybody; a published table must not
link to one`. After removing the HREF, the same audit returned zero findings.
