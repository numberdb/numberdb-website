## `UniversalCyclotomicField()` needs libgap, which named imports cannot load; a `CyclotomicField` does the same job

What happened: to count the elements of a hyperbolic Coxeter group by
length, I built its Tits representation over
`UniversalCyclotomicField()`, the field Sage's own `CoxeterGroup` uses. Under
`import numberdb.sage` and named imports the constructor raised
`ImportError: cannot import name libgap`, from `late_import()` in
`universal_cyclotomic_field.py`. Importing `sage.libs.gap.libgap` directly
first fails with the same error, so importing it earlier does not help.
`CyclotomicField(N)` with $N=2\,\mathrm{lcm}(m_{ij})$, and
$2\cos(\pi/m)=z^{N/2m}+z^{-N/2m}$, has no GAP dependency. It gave the
$(2,3,7)$, $[8,3]$ and $[\infty,3]$ growth series exactly.

What the skill says now: "Named imports don't bring root finding
(`sage.numerical.optimize`), `RealBall.str`, or `QQ('1.25')`". Nothing on
GAP-backed parents.

What it should say: add that `UniversalCyclotomicField` fails under named
imports with "cannot import name libgap", because it loads GAP lazily. So
does anything built on it by default; I did not test `CoxeterGroup` or
`WeylGroup` themselves. For a reflection representation, use
`CyclotomicField(2*lcm)` explicitly.

Evidence: 2026-09-12. `/tmp/growth.py` gave the traceback ending in
`sage/libs/gap/element.pyx ... ImportError: cannot import name libgap`.
`/tmp/libgap_probe.py` printed `UCF fails: ImportError cannot import name
libgap` and `direct libgap import fails: ImportError cannot import name
libgap`. `/tmp/steinberg.py`, over `CyclotomicField`, printed the
$[7,3]$ denominator as Lehmer's polynomial.

## Test a published closed form against the shape its theory forces before transcribing it

What happened: arXiv:1907.00595 (Kozma–Szirmai, Geom. Dedicata 2023) gives
the volumes of the 14 non-compact Coxeter simplices in $\mathbb H^6$ to
$\mathbb H^9$ as closed forms. Its row for $\overline P_8=[3,3^{[8]}]$ reads
$17\pi^3/285768000$. In even dimension $n$, Gauss–Bonnet makes a hyperbolic
volume $(-1)^{n/2}\chi\,\mathrm{vol}(S^n)/2$ with $\chi$ rational, so in
dimension 8 it is a rational multiple of $\pi^4$. The printed $\pi^3$ cannot
be right, whatever the correct rational is, and both neighbouring rows
($\overline S_8$, $\overline Q_8$) are $17\pi^4/\ldots$. The same source's
[3,3,6] and [3,4,4] rows, $\tfrac18\Lambda(\pi/3)$ and
$\tfrac16\Lambda(\pi/4)$, do match an independent computation: a quarter of
the Bianchi covolumes from Humbert's formula, to $10^{-100}$.

What the skill says now: "Check against something independent", and "When a
recomputation disagrees with a stored value, suspect the recomputation
first." Nothing on checking a source before it is used.

What it should say: a closed form copied from a paper is a transcription of
a transcription, since the survey tables cite the original. Before building
on it, check the structure its theory forces: the transcendental factor
Gauss–Bonnet requires, the field a value must lie in, the degree of an
algebraic number. A printed exponent that breaks that structure is a
misprint, and a checker comparing digits it derived from the same misprint
would agree with it.

Evidence: 2026-09-12. `curl -L https://arxiv.org/e-print/1907.00595`,
`69dim_Kozma_Sz-arXiv2.tex` line 812:
`$[3,3^{[8]}]$ & $\overline{P}_8$ & $17 \pi^3/285768000$`, with lines 788,
834 and 855 giving $\pi^4$ for the other dimension-8 rows. The Bianchi
comparison is `/tmp/checks.py`: `vol(-3)/4 : [0.0422892336004…]`,
`vol(-4)/4 - G/12` zero to the ball radius.

## `matrix(RealField(prec), ...)` fails under named imports until `sage.rings.polynomial.laurent_polynomial_ring` is imported

What happened: after `import numberdb.sage` and named imports,
`matrix(RealField(200), 3, 3, ...)` raised `AttributeError: module
'sage.rings.polynomial' has no attribute 'laurent_polynomial_ring'`, from
`get_matrix_class` in `matrix_space.py`. That function tests the base ring
against `LaurentPolynomialRing_mpair` by attribute access on a submodule
nobody has imported. The same constructor over a `CyclotomicField` and over
`RealBallField(100)` worked. After `import
sage.rings.polynomial.laurent_polynomial_ring` the real matrix was built,
and its `charpoly()` came out right.

What the skill says now: "Named imports don't bring root finding
(`sage.numerical.optimize`), `RealBall.str`, or `QQ('1.25')`". Nothing on
matrices.

What it should say: add matrices over `RealField`, with the one-line import
that fixes them. Or say to prefer `RealBallField` matrices, which also
carry their error.

Evidence: 2026-09-12, `/tmp/rr_matrix_probe.py`: `RR matrix fails:
AttributeError module 'sage.rings.polynomial' has no attribute
'laurent_polynomial_ring'`, then `RR matrix ok after importing
laurent_polynomial_ring; charpoly x^2 - 5.000…*x - 2.000…`, and `RBF matrix
ok -2.000…`.
