## Check trace and determinant from the characteristic polynomial, not by multiplying algebraic roots

What happened: the eigenvalue generator for T329 first verified
$\sum_k\lambda_k=\operatorname{tr}A$ and
$\prod_k\lambda_k=\det A$ by adding and multiplying the roots after coercing
them to Sage's algebraic real field `AA`. The dry run reached the Hilbert
matrix at $n=5$ and then spent minutes proving the product identity. Moving
the same check to the characteristic polynomial over $\mathbb{Q}$ made the
full proposed range, $2\leq n\leq12$ over 11 matrix families, finish in about
10 seconds.

What the skill says now: check every identity before writing it down, and
verify against something outside the family. It does not say that an identity
which is cheap over the exact polynomial may be expensive after the roots are
split into algebraic numbers.

What it should say: when checking eigenvalues or polynomial roots, verify
symmetric identities such as trace, determinant, sum of roots or product of
roots from the polynomial coefficients before root isolation. Use the
algebraic roots for identities that really name individual roots, such as
closed forms for each eigenvalue or reciprocal-pair relations.

Evidence: the first `agents/table-build/dry_run.py` run on
`generators/classical-test-matrix-eigenvalues/generate.py` was interrupted
after more than six minutes. A timing driver showed the stall at `hilbert,
n=5`. After the generator checked the $x^{n-1}$ and constant coefficients of
`charpoly()` instead of `sum(roots)` and `prod(roots)`, the same dry run
computed all 847 entries; its longest value was 150 characters and the entries
block was 163.7 KB.
