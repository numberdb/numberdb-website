## An odd number of `$` in a field passes the audit and breaks the page

What happened: T296's comment on $r=10$ reads
`$K(k_{10})=\sqrt{2+3\sqrt2+\sqrt5}$ \sqrt{\Gamma(1/40)…/(2560\pi^3)}$`. That
is three dollars. The mathematics closes early, the Gamma product prints as
raw TeX, and a stray `$` is left on the line. `audit_table` reported the table
clean. The other nine comments, which a generator built in the same way, were
fine, so reading a few samples did not catch it.

What the skill says now: use `$...$` for mathematics. It says nothing about
checking that the dollars balance.

What it should say: before sending, count the `$` in every prose field and
every entry comment. An odd count is always a fault. It is one line in a
generator (`assert text.count('$') % 2 == 0`), and it catches a fault that
looks fine in the YAML and is visible only on the page.

Evidence: `GET /api/table?id=T296`, 2026-09-17: comment counts
`[2,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,3]` for $r=1..10$; `GET /api/table/T296/audit` returned
`"clean": true`.

## A family that converges to a constant fills search with that constant

What happened: T296 holds $K(k_r)$ for $1\leq r\leq100$. Because
$K(k_r)=\frac{\pi}{2}(1+4e^{-\pi\sqrt r}+\dots)$, 77 of the 100 rows agree with
$\pi/2$ to a relative $10^{-6}$, 61 to $10^{-8}$ and 40 to $10^{-10}$. A reader
who types $\pi/2$ to six or eight places will get dozens of these rows beside
$K(0)$, and hardly anybody arrives holding $K(k_{73})$. The range was chosen to
match a companion table.

What the skill says now: values that are a handful of small integers "match
everything and tell nobody anything". It also says to hold the numbers that
turn up and to justify the range in the completeness note.

What it should say: the same holds for a family that tends to a limit. Past
the index where the rows agree with the limit to the digits a reader would
type, the rows answer searches for the limit and inform none of them. Measure
where that index is, and stop there unless the literature quotes the later
values.

Evidence: `agents/sage.sh /tmp/t296_checks.py`, 2026-09-17, printed
`rows within 1e-06 relative of pi/2: 77`, `1e-08: 61` and `1e-10: 40`. T25
holds $K(0)=\pi/2$.
