## Use ball methods for `psi`, and get Euler's constant as `-psi(1)`
What happened: checking T228's reflection formula needed the digamma function
and Euler's constant in arb. `ComplexBallField` elements have `.psi()`, so
`field(s).psi()` worked. `ComplexBallField` itself had no
`.euler_constant()`, and `from sage.functions.other import psi` failed in
the named-import environment with a circular symbolic import. The check used
`-field(1).psi()` for Euler's constant and stayed in ball arithmetic.

What the skill says now: it recommends ball arithmetic and warns that named
imports do not bring every Sage helper, but it does not say how to get
digamma or Euler's constant without falling back to symbolic Sage.

What it should say: when an arb computation needs the digamma function, call
`.psi()` on a real or complex ball element. For Euler's constant in the same
field, use `-field(1).psi()` rather than looking for `field.euler_constant()`
or importing symbolic `psi`.

Evidence: T228 repair, 2026-09-13. `/tmp/probe_sage_methods.py` under
`agents/sage.sh` showed `ComplexBallField(prec)(1/3).psi()` works,
`ComplexBallField(prec).euler_constant()` is absent, and
`from sage.functions.other import psi` raised an AttributeError from Sage's
symbolic import path.
