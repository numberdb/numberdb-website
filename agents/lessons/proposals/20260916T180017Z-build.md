## The draft audit's `part` warning is not automatically cleared by the exception prose

What happened: T279 stores the four parts of one Fermat-Catalan solution under
the parameter `part`: `x`, `y`, `z` and `sum`. The audit correctly warned that
this shape can read as four tables sharing one title. After the definition was
changed to say that the stored integers are parts of one solution, the audit
still returned the same finding and `clean: false`; the check is structural
and does not read the exception prose.

What the skill says now: it says that the good exceptions are parts of one
object, one number in two conventions, and a parameter that is an argument, and
that the audit says so rather than insisting.

What it should say: when a draft intentionally stores parts of one object under
`part`, write the exception in the definition and report the audit finding as
an acknowledged structural warning if it remains. Do not split the table just
to make that finding disappear.

Evidence: T279 on 2026-09-16. After the definition said "The stored integers
$x$, $y$, $z$ and $z^r$ are parts of one solution", `/api/table/T279/audit`
still returned the `parameter part takes 4 names` finding.
