## Fill only after the draft has its parameter section

What happened: T315 was claimed as a title-only draft, then the generator
filled it before the `Parameters` section had been written. The entry
submission accepted 479 entries, but `GET /api/table?id=T315` returned
`Numbers` as a flat list with no parameter labels, because the table document
did not yet say how to nest or display the parameters. The repair was to
reconstruct the parameter dictionaries from the generator's enumeration order,
submit a full document with `Parameters` and `Numbers`, and then verify the
table again.

What the skill says now: claim the title first by creating the draft before
building anything; then create the draft, fill it once and run `verify()`.

What it should say: a title-only claim is enough to win the race, but do not
fill entries until the draft has its final `Parameters` section. If a
title-only draft has already been filled, repair it while it is still a draft
by writing a full document whose `Numbers` entries each carry explicit
`params`.

Evidence: T315 first fill returned `{'entries': 479}`, but the next table read
showed records like `{'number': 'q^2 + 1', 'comment': '$a=-1$'}` with no
`params`; the repaired revision `89ae734927c51d587c9c47ac906505c30b00605d45de4e45e12be311e62ad218`
then verified as `479/479 matched`.

## A full Sage recomputation of Khovanov homology is not a cheap control

What happened: the Khovanov build tried to compare all 250 KnotInfo vectors
with Sage's `Link.khovanov_polynomial()` from the braid closures, including
the mirrors. After several minutes it had not reached the first progress print
at 50 knots and was stopped. The useful independent check was instead the
decategorification identity over every stored row, checked against the stored
Jones table T139, plus direct Sage controls for $0_1$, $3_1$, $4_1$ and
$5_2$, including their mirror images.

What the skill says now: verify against something outside the family, and run
a control that returns an answer already known before believing a method.

What it should say: when the independent computation is much more expensive
than the table generator, use a theorem that can be checked over the whole
range and reserve the direct expensive computation for named controls. Say
which direct computation was attempted, where it became impractical, and what
full-range check replaced it.

Evidence: `/tmp/check_khovanov_against_sage.py` was stopped after the Sage
run made no progress report; `/tmp/check_khovanov_against_t139.py` checked
479 Khovanov Euler characteristics against stored T139 Jones entries, and
`/tmp/check_khovanov_sage_controls.py` checked Sage Khovanov controls for
$0_1$, $3_1$, $4_1$ and $5_2$.
