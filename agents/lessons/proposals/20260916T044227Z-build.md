## Group tableau checks by shape before checking every Kostka-Foulkes coefficient

What happened: the first independent check for T259 recomputed
`SemistandardTableaux(lambda, evaluation=mu)` separately for every
`(lambda, mu)` pair. It reached the large size-9 cases and was still running
after thirteen minutes. The same check finished in about two minutes when it
generated `SemistandardTableaux(lambda, max_entry=|lambda|)` once per shape,
grouped the tableaux by their partition weight, and accumulated both
`t^charge(T)` and the ordinary tableau count for every `mu` in that one pass.

What the skill says now: check every identity before writing it down, and
verify against something outside the generator.

What it should say: when an independent check counts or weights
combinatorial objects indexed by several parameters, look first for a way to
generate the objects once by a coarser parameter and group them by the rest.
For Kostka-Foulkes polynomials, a full-range charge check should group
semistandard tableaux by shape and partition weight rather than regenerate the
same shape separately for each weight.

Evidence: `/tmp/kostka_identity_checks.py` began a per-pair charge check over
901 entries and was stopped after thirteen minutes. `/tmp/kostka_identity_checks_grouped.py`
checked the same 901 entries, the Schur-to-Hall-Littlewood transition
dictionaries, the charge sums, the ordinary Kostka specialisation at `t=1`,
and the dominance-zero condition with `problems: 0`.
