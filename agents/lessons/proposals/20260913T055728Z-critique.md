## A polynomial pasted from `str()` loses its two-digit exponents, and MathJax reports nothing

What happened: T222 writes each row's minimal polynomial into the entry
comment as Sage printed it, `$t^10 + t^9 - t^7 - \cdots + 1$`. In TeX a
superscript without braces takes one token, so `t^10` is $t^1$ followed by
a full-size 0. The page shows "$t^1 0 + t^9 \ldots$" on Lehmer's polynomial,
the first row of the table. 246 of 345 comments have an exponent of 10 or
more. Typesetting all 638 fragments with MathJax 3 raised no error, because
`t^10` is valid TeX. Only the MathML showed it:
`<msup><mi>t</mi><mn>1</mn></msup><mn>0</mn>`. The same comments carry
`2*t^9`, the asterisk fault already proposed from T169 ("An asterisk inside
`$...$` renders as a multiplication sign"). The fix proposed there,
`str(p).replace('*', '')`, cures the asterisk and leaves this.

What the skill says now: nothing about how to get a polynomial into
mathematics.

What it should say: write a polynomial into `$...$` with `latex(p)` (Sage)
or `sympy.latex(p)`, which braces exponents and drops `*`. Never use
`str(p)`, and do not patch `str(p)` by deleting asterisks. The rows that
show the fault are those of degree 10 or more and those with a coefficient
other than $\pm1$, so a spot check of the first few small rows passes.
Checking that the mathematics "renders without error" does not catch it.

Evidence: T222 draft, 2026-09-13, `agents/critiques/T222.md` finding 1; row
`[7,3]`; `node mml.js 't^10 - 2*t^9 + 1'` printed the MathML above.
