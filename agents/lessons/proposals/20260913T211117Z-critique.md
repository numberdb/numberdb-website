## An integer formatted into a TeX subscript or superscript needs braces, and the fault shows only from 10 on

What happened: T236's generator writes each row's label as
`r"$\operatorname{Vol}(B_%d)$" % n`. For $n\leq9$ this is right. For $n=10$
it is `B_10`, which TeX reads as $B_1$ followed by a full-size 0. MathJax
raised no error, and its MathML was
`<msub><mi>B</mi><mn>1</mn></msub><mn>0</mn>`. The table runs to $n=10$,
so only its last three rows show the fault, and those rows hold the value a
reader most wants. The T222 proposal (`20260913T055728Z-critique.md`) found
the same fault in `t^10` from a polynomial's `str()`, and its fix, `latex(p)`,
does not reach a label assembled by hand. The sibling generator for T232
writes `$H_%d(t)$` and is safe only because that table stops at $n=6$.
Extending it past 9 would break it silently.

What the skill says now: nothing about building mathematics from a format
string.

What it should say: whenever a program puts a number into `$...$`, brace it:
`B_{%d}`, `t^{%d}`, `f"B_{{{n}}}"` in an f-string (where a bare `{n}` is
the substitution and leaves no brace in the output). Check the row with the
largest index, not the first. "Renders without error" does not catch this,
because `B_10` is valid TeX.

Evidence: `GET /api/table?id=T236`, 2026-09-13, rows `10/relative`,
`10/normalised` and `10/euclidean`;
`generators/birkhoff-polytope-volumes/generate.py` lines 160, 165 and 170.
`/tmp/crit221_mj/c236_mml.js` printed the MathML above.
`agents/critiques/T236.md`, finding 1.
