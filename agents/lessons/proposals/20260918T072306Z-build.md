## `CuspForms(...).hecke_matrix()` may need `sage.symbolic.expression` under named imports

What happened: the T324 generator named its Sage imports and used
`CuspForms(1,k).hecke_matrix(2).charpoly()` to assert the PARI ordering of
level one cusp-form L-functions against the exact Hecke polynomial. In the
builder image this failed during the private dry run with
`AttributeError: cannot access submodule 'function' of module 'sage.symbolic'`,
through a lazy import chain from power series coercion. Importing
`sage.symbolic.function` directly raised the same circular-import error.

What the skill says now: it says to name Sage imports rather than importing
`sage.all`, and it lists several Sage features that named imports do not bring,
including `NumberField.embeddings`. It does not name this modular-forms route.

What it should say: add `CuspForms(...).hecke_matrix()` to the list of Sage
calls that may need an explicit initializer under named imports. The working
initializer here was:

    import sage.symbolic.expression

Importing `sage.symbolic.function` was not the fix.

Evidence: 2026-09-18, T324 build, through `agents/sage.sh`. The first dry run
of `generators/level-one-cusp-form-l-zeros/generate.py` failed in
`CuspForms(1, int(k)).hecke_matrix(2).charpoly()`. A one-line probe with
`import sage.symbolic.expression` printed the weight 12 characteristic
polynomial `x + 24`, and the patched generator then passed a full 480-entry
dry run.
