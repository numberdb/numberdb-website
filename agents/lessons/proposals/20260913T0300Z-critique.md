## Name a family in prose or in symbols, but do not give one family a symbol and the rest none

What happened: T220's definition read "the classical projective linear,
unitary, symplectic and orthogonal groups, the exceptional Chevalley and
Steinberg groups, the Suzuki and Ree groups, and the Tits group
${}^2F_4(2)'$". Sixteen families named in words, one in a symbol. A reader
meets a single piece of notation with nothing to compare it against and no
reason for it being there, and the natural question -- where are the others? --
has an answer the definition does not give: every family's symbol is the
display value of the `family` parameter, and the Tits group's own symbol
appears again in `comment-exclusions`.

The builder had a reason for the odd one out, and it was a good one: the Tits
group is the single entry that is not a family at all, but one group, the
derived subgroup of the non-simple ${}^2F_4(2)$. It reached for the symbol to
distinguish it. But that distinction was already made two fields below, in the
comment that lists the non-simple cases and says "the Tits group ${}^2F_4(2)'$
is included as its own row".

What the skill says now: "A title is what a search reaches" covers titles, and
the audit reports a definition longer than the median. Neither catches a
definition that is internally inconsistent at normal length.

What it should say: within one field, a family of things should be named one
way. If the symbols matter, they belong where the reader can compare them --
the parameter's `values`, which exist for exactly this -- and the definition
names the families in words. If one member needs distinguishing, the
distinction is a comment, where it can be explained rather than hinted at with
notation.

The general form: prose that reaches for notation in one place is usually
prose that is missing a sentence somewhere else. Ask what the symbol is doing
there, and whether the thing it is doing is already done elsewhere on the page.

Evidence: numberdb.org/T220, built 2026-09-12 and read by a person the same
evening, who asked "only the Tits group has a symbol, shall we write the
symbols in a coherent place?". The campaign's own critique stage had read the
table and not raised it. The fix removed the symbol from the definition and
changed nothing else: the seventeen symbols under `family` and the sentence in
`comment-exclusions` were already correct and already in the right places.
