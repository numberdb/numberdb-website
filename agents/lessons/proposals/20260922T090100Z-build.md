## Parse rational text by integers, not by `QQ(text)`

What happened: an external check for the quartic oscillator perturbation
coefficients parsed Sergeev's exact coefficient files with `QQ(value)`, where
`value` was text such as `"-21/8"`. Under `agents/sage.sh`, after
`import numberdb.sage` and named Sage imports, that raised
`AttributeError: module 'sage.rings' has no attribute 'real_mpfr'`. Splitting
the text at `/` and building `QQ(int(numerator)) / QQ(int(denominator))`
worked.

What the skill says now: it warns that `QQ('1.25')` is not available with
named imports and says to build a rational from a decimal string by hand.

What it should say: parse every rational that arrives as text by its integer
parts, not with `QQ(text)`. This applies to exact fraction strings such as
`3/4` as well as decimal strings.

Evidence: `/tmp/check_quartic_rs_against_sources.py` first failed on
`QQ(value)` while parsing `anhq_0.m`; after explicit numerator and denominator
parsing, the check could compare the source coefficients.
