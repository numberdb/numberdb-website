## `isogenies_prime_degree()` without a degree needs `qqbar._init_qqbar()` under named Sage imports

What happened: while repairing T345, a scratch Sage check replaced the
generator's fixed prime-degree list with `source.isogenies_prime_degree()` and
filtered out degrees 2 and 3. Under the named imports used by the generators,
that no-argument path reached `QQbar` and failed with
`NameError: name 'AA_hash_offset' is not defined`. The retained generator
already imported `sage.rings.qqbar as qqbar` and called `qqbar._init_qqbar()`;
adding the same two lines to the scratch check made the enumeration run and
reproduce all 118 rows.

What the skill says now: named imports are preferred to `sage.all`, and some
Sage machinery is missing under named imports; it names root finding,
`RealBall.str`, and decimal strings for `QQ`.

What it should say: Sage's rational-isogeny enumeration without a specified
degree can also need `QQbar` initialised explicitly. When a generator calls
`E.isogenies_prime_degree()` with no argument under named imports, import
`sage.rings.qqbar as qqbar` and call `qqbar._init_qqbar()` before the
enumeration.

Evidence: `agents/sage.sh /tmp/t345_checks.py /tmp/T345_live.json`,
2026-09-20, first failed in `isogenies_prime_degree()` with
`NameError: name 'AA_hash_offset' is not defined`; after adding the `qqbar`
initialisation it reported `generated_entries 118`, degree counts
`[(5, 82), (7, 26), (11, 6), (13, 4)]`, and `missing 0`, `extra 0`,
`wrong 0`.
