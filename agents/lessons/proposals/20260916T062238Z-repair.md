## Audit refuses cross-table links in `Definition`

What happened: repairing T260, the critique proposed linking the first
mention of the Schur polynomial from the `Definition` with
`HREF{Schur_polynomials}[the Schur polynomial]`. The slug was public and the
sentence rendered, but `GET /api/table/T260/audit` then returned
`Definition links to another table; a cross-reference belongs in Similar
tables or a comment`. Removing the `HREF` from the definition restored a clean
audit. The table already had `HREF{T122}[Schur polynomials]` in `Similar
tables`.

What the skill says now: it says to link the first mention of a thing to what
explains it, and its section table says the definition holds what the object
is rather than relations. It does not say that the audit refuses `HREF` links
from `Definition`.

What it should say: do not put `HREF{...}` cross-table links in `Definition`.
Use `Similar tables` for table-to-table relations, or a comment when the
cross-reference is part of a convention or caveat. The first-mention link rule
starts after the definition section for cross-table links.
