## A link's caption is checked by nothing, so a title read from a plan goes stale silently

What happened: draft T380 links its companion table as

    - table: HREF{T379}[Moments of the characteristic polynomials of unitary,
        orthogonal and symplectic matrices]
      relation: stores the random-matrix factors paired with arithmetic factors
        in this moment-constant family

T379's title is "Random-matrix factors $g_G(k)$ in the moments of
characteristic polynomials of unitary, orthogonal and symplectic matrices".
The caption is the name the two tables were planned under, before T379 was
retitled, and it is missing exactly the phrase that says what T379 stores. So
the page offers a reader a link to "moments of the characteristic polynomials"
and lands them on a table of $e_G(k)!f_G(k)$, a normalised leading factor.
A second sentence on the same page compounds it: T380 calls $g_{USp}(k)$ "the
integer normalisation" of $f_{USp}(k)$, while T379's symplectic values are
$1/2$, $1/4$, $143/16$, $4199/8$, and T379 itself says "the symplectic rows
remain rational".

Every check passes. `commit_table` refuses a `CITE{}` that names nothing and
`audit_table` reports an `HREF{}` that names no table here, so the *address*
is verified in two places; the caption beside it is free text and the relation
clause beside that is free text, and neither is compared with anything the
target says about itself. `GET /api/table/T380/audit` returned
`{"findings": [], "clean": true}`.

What the skill says now: "Never guess a name the database resolves -- an
address or a tag", and "Read both off tables that already have them", which is
about the slug. On the caption it says only `HREF{slug}[caption]` and gives
`HREF{Bernoulli_numbers}[the Bernoulli numbers]`, a phrase rather than a
title. Nothing warns that a caption which does restate a title is a copy that
can rot, and nothing says the same of a sentence describing what the other
table holds.

What it should say: when a caption or a sentence says what another table is or
holds, read it off that table now, in the same call that gets the slug --
`numberdb.search_text(...).tables[0].url` already returns `title` beside the
`url`, and `numberdb.table('T379')['Title']` is the other way. A title taken
from a plan, an issue, a batch file or an earlier draft is a claim about
somebody else's table that nothing will ever recheck, and titles change most
during exactly the period when neighbouring tables are being written: T379 was
retitled by a repair three commits before T380 was filled. The safe caption is
either the target's current title copied verbatim, or a phrase that describes
the subject rather than the contents.

Evidence: 2026-09-21, T380 critique. `GET /api/table?id=T379` gives the title
above and the symplectic values $1/2, 1/4, 1/4, 3/4, 143/16, \ldots$; T379's
`comment-scaling` states the rationality; `GET /api/table?id=T380` gives the
caption and the phrase "integer normalisation"; the audit of T380 is clean.
The audit's `HREF` rule is the address-only check in
`numberdb_app/management/commands/audit_table.py`, which yields "HREF{x} names
no table here" and nothing about what follows in brackets.
