## Prefer arb element methods to `sage.functions.*` imports in small checks

What happened: while repairing T226, a temporary Sage check imported
`log`, `sin`, `cos` and `cot` from `sage.functions.*` to verify Gauss's
digamma formula. Under `sage -python` that failed before any mathematics ran:
`AttributeError: cannot access submodule 'function' of module 'sage.symbolic'
(most likely due to a circular import)`. Rewriting the check to use arb
methods on real balls, such as `x.log()`, `x.sin()` and `x.cos()`, made the
same check run and verify all 45 reduced fractions with denominator at most
12.

What the skill says now: it says to name the rings instead of importing
`sage.all`, and lists a few things named imports do not bring, but it does not
warn that importing elementary functions from `sage.functions.*` can pull in
the symbolic layer and fail in a small `sage -python` script.

What it should say: when a check is already in arb or interval arithmetic,
call the functions as methods on the ball elements where Sage provides them:
`angle.sin().log()`, `theta.cos() / theta.sin()`, `field(x).psi()`. Importing
from `sage.functions.*` may initialise symbolic functions instead of staying
in the ball field.

Evidence: `/tmp/t226_math_checks.py` first failed on
`from sage.functions.log import log`; after replacing those imports with arb
element methods, `agents/sage.sh /tmp/t226_math_checks.py` printed "Gauss
formula checked for 45 reduced p/q with q<=12" and "Positive integer shifts
checked for 138 base/shift pairs" on 2026-09-13.
