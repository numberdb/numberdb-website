## Shortening a definition by moving its formula into `Formulas` leaves a name, not a definition
What happened: T299's Definition once gave
$\alpha(r)=\pi/(4K(m_r)^2)+\sqrt r-\sqrt r\,E(m_r)/K(m_r)$. It was shortened to
"The entry is Ramanujan's elliptic alpha function $\alpha(r)$, in the
singular-modulus convention of CITE{MathWorldAlpha}", and the formula became
`formula-elliptic-integrals`. The page then had no definition a reader could
compute from. It set $\tau$ and $q$ and never used them. Two `Similar tables`
relations still said their tables held quantities "used in the definition",
and the definition no longer mentioned those quantities. A comment kept using
$m_r$ and $k_r$, which were now defined only further down the page.

What the skill says now: the Definition holds "one or two sentences saying
what the object *is*", with no caveats, history or relations. It does not
say that a formula can *be* the definition, or that a citation standing in
for the formula is not a definition.

What it should say: when a Definition is too long, shorten it by removing
what is not the definition (conventions, relations, caveats), not by
removing the formula that says what the entry is. "Function X in the
convention of [source]" names the object and sends the reader elsewhere; two
people could not build the same table from it. After any shortening, search
the rest of the table for "the definition" and for symbols the Definition
used to introduce.

Evidence: T299 on 2026-09-17, commit ecfb279 "shorten elliptic alpha
definition"; critique at `agents/critiques/T299.md`, finding 1. `audit_table`
reported the table clean.
