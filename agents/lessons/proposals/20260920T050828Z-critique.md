## A clean audit says nothing about `Links`: nothing checks that a link is cited

What happened: the critique of draft T359 found 29 entries in `Links`, of which
28 read "Requested in numberdb-data#N" and pointed at closed issues asking for
entirely different tables -- Ramsey numbers, knot polynomials, volumes of
hyperbolic manifolds. No sentence in the document cites any of them; the page
renders them as numbered links `[2]` to `[29]` under the mathematics.
`GET /api/table/T359/audit` reported neither them nor anything else about
`Links`: its two findings were both about tags.

That is not an oversight in one draft's audit. The audit reads `Links` twice,
and neither read is about whether anything points at a link: once to report a
link whose *title* contains another table's title (prefer `HREF{}`), and once,
under `--links`, to fetch each URL and report a bad status. A link that nothing
cites is valid, reachable, unique to this table, and invisible to every rule
there is. The same is not true of citations in the other direction:
`commit_table` refuses a `CITE{}` that names nothing, so an author learns
immediately about a dangling citation and never about an uncited link.

What the skill says now: "A reference is for citing, not for listing.
`CITE{Koshy}` in a comment earns its place; a bibliography nobody points at is
furniture." And `Links` holds "sources outside: Wikipedia, LMFDB, OEIS,
MathWorld", not "anything the database holds itself". Both are right and both
are unenforced, and section 10 ("Check the work") tells a contributor to let
`audit_table` confirm the corpus-facing details, which invites reading a clean
audit as a clean `Links`.

What it should say: the audit checks that citations resolve, not that links are
cited, so read `Links` and `References` by hand before offering a draft. Every
entry should be pointed at from the prose with `CITE{}`, or be the one or two
outward sources a reader of this subject would want; anything else is
furniture the audit will not remove for you. The same reading catches the
request link the skill already forbids -- the issue that asked for the table is
answered in the issue, and the table carries no trace of it.

Evidence: T359 on 2026-09-20. `GET /api/table/T359/audit` with the draft
author's key returned `clean: false` with two tag findings and nothing about
`Links`; `GET /api/table?id=T359` showed 29 links against 3 `CITE{}` keys
(`NashWiki`, `CarlenLoss1993`, `HaberlSchuster2009`); the 28 issue titles were
read from `api.github.com/repos/numberdb/numberdb-data/issues/N` and none
concerns Nash's inequality. The audit's only `Links` rules are at
`numberdb_app/management/commands/audit_table.py:373` and `:465`. The one-link
form of the same fault was finding 1 of `agents/critiques/T354.md` and finding 3
of `agents/critiques/T339.md`.
