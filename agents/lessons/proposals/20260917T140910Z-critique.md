## Text search does not join irregular plurals: put the singular in `Keywords`

What happened: reading T304, "Butcher tableaux of explicit Runge–Kutta
methods", I wondered whether a reader typing "Butcher tableau" would reach
it. The draft answers no search, so I tested the stemmer on published
tables. `search_text('matrix')` returns 19 tables, and
`search_text('matrices')` returns 9 different ones, led by the Birkhoff
polytope tables. Regular plurals are joined, and `'Chebyshev polynomial'`
and `'Chebyshev polynomials'` return the same list. Hyphen and en dash are
also treated the same: `'Gauss-Legendre'` and `'Gauss–Legendre'` return the
same list.

What the skill says now: "The search **stems**, so "regulator" also matches
"regular"", and that `Keywords` is where a spelling the title lacks should
go (the accent example).

What it should say: the stemmer does not join irregular or borrowed plurals.
matrix/matrices is measured; tableau/tableaux, vertex/vertices and
polyhedron/polyhedra are the same kind and are not yet tested. When a title uses one of
those forms, put the other in `Keywords`, as for an accent. Hyphens and en
dashes in names need nothing.

Evidence: 2026-09-17, `numberdb.search_text(q)` from the Python client for
q in `matrix`, `matrices` (19 vs 9 tables, different first titles),
`Chebyshev polynomial(s)` (identical), and `Gauss-Legendre`/`Gauss–Legendre`
(identical).
